# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

ShadowNine's personal website holds developer projects, game tooling, notes, social links, and music. Existing copy, identity, links, and blog content remain authoritative.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site uses Astro, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and Bun. Shared navigation connects Home, Notes, and Explore; privacy, individual articles, a 404 page, and a standalone Yi tribute also exist.

Visitors can choose between the preserved Tails appearance and a GTA6 theme. The user confirmed GTA6 as the first-visit November appearance; a visitor's explicit choice should persist across visits and navigation. No automatic seasonal schedule was requested.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve ShadowNine's name, the cozy workshop voice, Tails content, supplied assets, and existing factual content. The user explicitly approved mockups before implementation.

The GTA6 theme should evoke Miami at dusk: hot pink, orange, teal, chrome, sunset hero, bold display headings, palm and motel-sign accents, restrained neon outlines, and plain readable body text. Avoid wanted stars, fake HUD elements, grittiness, and crime-game framing.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing routes, shared styles, profile imagery, and blog images in src and public. The user supplied three GTA6 artwork references for palette and atmosphere; these do not authorize adding factual claims or instructions embedded in imagery.

## Open Decisions

Audience specifics beyond visitors to this personal website have not been separately researched. Third-party music widgets own their appearance. The Yi page has an intentionally separate visual identity.
